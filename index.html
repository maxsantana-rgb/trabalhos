Simple Omelette Recipe

An easy and quick dish, perfect for any meal. This classic omelette combines beaten eggs cooked to perfection, optionally filled with your choice of cheese, vegetables, or meats.

Preparation Time

  • Total: Approximately 10 minutes
  • Preparation: 5 minutes
  • Cooking: 5 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2–3 large eggs
  • Salt, to taste
  • Pepper, to taste
  • 1 tablespoon of butter or oil
  • Optional fillings: cheese, vegetables, cooked meats, herbs

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium heat.
  3. Pour the eggs into the pan.
  4. When the eggs begin to cook, lift the edges with a spatula.
  5. Add your preferred fillings.
  6. Fold the omelette and cook for another minute.
  7. Serve while still hot.
